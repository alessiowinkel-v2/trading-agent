**Total**: $X,XXX.XX
**% of portfolio**: X.X%

**Thesis**: (2-3 sentences on why)
**Catalyst**: (specific reason for timing)
**Risk**: (what would make this wrong)
**Size rationale**: (why this size)
**Exit plan**: (sell conditions, both win and loss)

**Kill switch check**: (which were checked, all clear?)
**Strategy rules check**: (any rules under stress? document any close calls)
